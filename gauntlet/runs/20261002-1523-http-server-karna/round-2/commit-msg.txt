fix(http): validate unread request bodies and make close() deadlock-free

Before writing a non-streamed response, the server now reads and checks
any body the handler left unread, and sends 400/413 with Connection: close
on broken framing. close() drops its monitor in favour of a CAS plus latch
with bounded joins, which fixes the deadlock between an outside close and
one from a handler. The connection also lingers during a graceful stop.
