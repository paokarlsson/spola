feat(di): add spola.di explicit dependency injection container

Add a reflection-free DI container replacing Spring Core. Bindings declare
their dependencies via typed factory arities or a declared resolver list,
and Container.create validates the whole graph up front: missing
dependencies, duplicate keys and cycles with the full chain. Supports
qualifiers, lazy/eager singletons, prototype, ScopedValue call scope and
reverse-order closing of AutoCloseable components.
