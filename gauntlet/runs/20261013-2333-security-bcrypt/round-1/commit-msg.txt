feat(security): add BCrypt and a delegating {id} password hasher

BCryptPasswordHasher verifies $2a$/$2b$/$2y$ hashes from Spring and
writes $2a$ hashes Spring accepts; Blowfish is written out with its
tables derived from pi. PasswordHasher.delegating reads Spring's {id}
prefix. Passwords over 72 bytes or with U+0000 are refused, so long
passwords never collide (CVE-2025-22228). Tested against jBCrypt,
Openwall and libxcrypt vectors.
