docs(security): require the bcrypt cost to cover the stored cost

needsRehash never lowers a bcrypt cost, so a hasher below the stored
cost (defaults() against Spring's BCryptPasswordEncoder(12)) lets
known and unknown usernames be told apart by timing. The Javadoc,
README, PROGRESS and ARCHITECTURE now say to use at least the highest
stored cost. Also tidies cost limits, Blowfish key schedule and names.
