fix(web): keep a login's session owned and counted while it is renewed

A login no longer takes its session out of the user's index; the owner
is set in the same locked step as the renewal and the login holds the
session until its response, so a concurrent Sessions.renew can no
longer push a logged-in session past maximumSessions or let the next
user inherit it. The security-web Cache-Control copy is tested against
the same table, and lock order and names are tidied.
