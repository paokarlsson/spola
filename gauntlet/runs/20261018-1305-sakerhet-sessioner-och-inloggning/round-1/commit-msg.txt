fix(security-web): limit sessions per user and keep them out of caches

WebSecurity.Builder.maximumSessions(n) (default 10) ends a user's least
recently used session at login, so one account can no longer fill
maxSessions. A login over another user's session starts a fresh one.
Session- and XSRF-cookie responses and logged-in responses can no longer
be stored by a shared cache, and the cleanup at the limit runs once.
