fix(http): enforce TLS read deadlines and make PEM Base64 strict

Over TLS, a guard thread now closes the socket when a read runs past its
deadline. That covers the header, idle, body-read and linger timeouts,
so a client dripping one TLS record slowly can no longer hold a connection.
PEM bodies must have correct Base64 padding, so the flaky bad-length test
is now deterministic. Also fixes a permit leak in closeSocket after a
failed handshake, adds RSASSA-PSS keys, and cleans up sentinels and imports.
