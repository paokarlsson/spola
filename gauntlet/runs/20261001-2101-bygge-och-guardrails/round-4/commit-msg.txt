fix(guardrails): source-check launcher scripts without a .java suffix

Shebang scripts and other extension-less files that javac can parse now
go through the source check. jshell scripts and unparsable java shebang
scripts are reported as UNCHECKABLE_SOURCE. Unreadable zips count as jars,
and more build-file extensions are caught. Still open: CR-only shebangs,
non-UTF-8 and older-release sources slip past the check.
