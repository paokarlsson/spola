fix(util): keep errors from bad exceptions inside the console handler

Printing a stack trace whose toString or getMessage throws an Error,
such as an AssertionError or a StackOverflowError from a recursive
getMessage, no longer escapes the logging call when the console format
is installed; the record is logged with a marker line instead. U+2028
and U+2029 in the escape rule are now written as char escapes.
