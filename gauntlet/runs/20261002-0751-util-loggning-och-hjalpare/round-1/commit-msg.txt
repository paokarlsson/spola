feat(util): add spola.util logging, Lazy, Unchecked and ToString

New module spola.util replaces Lombok's @Slf4j, @Getter(lazy = true),
@SneakyThrows and @ToString.Exclude: a Log wrapper over System.Logger
with SLF4J-style {} placeholders that does no work when the level is
off, a one-line console format with per-prefix levels, a thread-safe
Lazy, checked-exception wrappers without sneaky throw, and a toString
builder that hides secrets.
