fix(util): stop exception messages from forging log records

Every stack-trace line in the console format now starts with a tab
and control characters such as U+2028 are escaped, so a newline in an
exception message can no longer produce a fake record at column 0.
Saved root-logger state is grouped in one record, and the duplicated
per-level methods in Log are documented as edit-together blocks.
