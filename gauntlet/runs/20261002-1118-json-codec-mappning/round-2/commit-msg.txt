fix(json): render declared field names in full in codec error paths

Codec problem paths now write declared field and discriminator names
exactly as spola.json's JsonAccessException does, so two long fields can
no longer share a cut-off path (round-1 MAJOR). Names that come from the
document (unknown members, map keys) are still cut at 40 characters.
Minor findings were addressed with documentation and comments.
