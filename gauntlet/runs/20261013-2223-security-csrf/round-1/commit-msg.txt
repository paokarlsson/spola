feat(security-web): protect against CSRF with XSRF-TOKEN

pattern: a readable XSRF-TOKEN cookie set on the first response, and
POST/PUT/PATCH/DELETE need a matching X-XSRF-TOKEN header or get 403
before any authentication. Login and logout get a new token. The need's
full flow runs with HttpClient and CookieManager. Only MINOR findings left.
