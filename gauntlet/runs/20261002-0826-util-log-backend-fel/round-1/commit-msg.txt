fix(util): keep backend logging failures from escaping Log

Every backend call in Log now goes through one place. If the backend
throws while logging an exception, Log logs the text once more without
the exception and adds a marker naming both error classes. Other backend
failures are swallowed, so the backend is called at most twice. Fatal VM
errors other than StackOverflowError are still rethrown, and that rule
now lives in one shared helper. Also fixes MNT-2 and MNT-3 from the last run.
